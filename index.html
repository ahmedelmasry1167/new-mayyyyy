Laminated or tempered?

Cars use two kinds of safety glass. Knowing which one a part is helps you order the right replacement.

Laminated glass

  • Two layers of glass bonded to a plastic (PVB) interlayer.
  • When hit, it cracks but stays in one piece, held together by the interlayer.
  • Can carry a tinted shade band across the top to cut glare.

Used for windshields. Catalog code LAM.

Tempered glass

  • A single sheet heated and rapidly cooled, which makes it much stronger than ordinary glass.
  • When it breaks, it shatters into small, blunt pieces instead of sharp shards.
  • Made on our TAMGLASS ScreenMax tempering line.

Used for door, side, vent and rear windows. Catalog code TEMP.
